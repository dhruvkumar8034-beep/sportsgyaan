Heritage Spotlight

The Pioneers & Legends of Indian Football

From the pioneering visionary who introduced the sport to the icons of the Golden Era, discover the history of Indian football.

HISTORIC ICON

Nagendra Prasad Sarbadhikari

The Father of Indian Football

Nagendra Prasad Sarbadhikari is revered across India for his visionary courage in bringing football to the subcontinent during the colonial British era. Inspired by British soldiers playing football in Kolkata's fields, he took the ball into his own hands, taught Indian youngsters, and founded indigenous football clubs that ignited a nationwide sporting revolution.

Founded Sovabazar Club (1887) Laid the cornerstone of Indian club football
Legendary Defender

Jarnail Singh

Known as the fearless defender of Indian football. His aerial supremacy, courage, and leadership anchored India to the Asian Games 1962 Gold Medal.

Master Tactician

Syed Abdul Rahim

The legendary coach who orchestrated India’s Golden Era in football. His revolutionary tactical genius led India to multiple international crowns and the 1956 Olympic semi-finals.

The Great Wall

Gostha Pal

Celebrated as the "Great Wall of China" for his impenetrable defensive bravery. He became the first barefoot captain of Mohun Bagan to conquer historic British teams.

Golden Captain

Chuni Goswami

The elegant forward and captain whose sublime dribbling, vision, and leadership captained India to glory at the 1962 Asian Games, acclaimed across Asia.
