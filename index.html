The Father of Indian Football - Nagendra Prasad Sarbadhikari

Nagendra Prasad Sarbadhikari is widely known as the Father of Indian Football, as he played a crucial role in introducing and popularizing the sport in India during the British era. Inspired by British soldiers playing football in Kolkata, he learned the game and began teaching it to Indian youth. His efforts led to the growth of football clubs and laid the foundation for India's football culture, making him one of the most important figures in the history of Indian sports.
